# Retro — 2026-08-31 · stage0c_literature

Stage 0c built `research-wiki/` from the 58 PDFs in `papers_Phylogeny/`, the Toro 2026
release archives, and the V4 record. 7/7 stop conditions met in ~2 h against a 4–8 h
estimate.

## What worked

- **Categorising before reading, with the rubric declared first.** Depth followed category,
  so 7 TOOL papers got two targeted extractions instead of full reads, and the budget went
  to the 21 BENCHMARK papers. Assigning from a mechanically extracted card (title,
  abstract, methods heading) and freezing the assignment before any full read kept the
  rubric from drifting into "papers we like".
- **Grounding TOOL in execution evidence, not citation counts.** Counting `.md` mentions
  said PADLOC 373, myRT 176, PhyML 11. Counting script invocations said PhyML was probed
  and **rejected**, and FoldMason was actually run. The second answer is the true one, and
  it changed two category assignments.
- **The verbatim gate earned its place.** `91_verify_verbatim.py` re-greps every quoted
  statement against its source. It failed twice on the first run — a `fi` ligature and a
  soft hyphen I had silently normalised while transcribing. Both were in *my* rendering,
  not the paper's. Without the gate, two claims would carry text no source contains.
- **Answering Toro §4b from the scripts.** The launcher's insistence on seed files over the
  abstract was the whole ballgame: one `--refs_fasta mestre_1928_refs.fasta` line settles a
  question no amount of reading the preprint's framing would have settled.

## What I got wrong

- **I declared a V4 mining rule that could not discriminate, and it went into an approved
  plan.** R1|R2 admitted 894 of 901 documents. The cause was obvious in hindsight: R2's
  tokens came from paper filenames, and in a retron project `retron`, `padloc`, `mestre`
  are subject vocabulary, present everywhere. **Ten minutes testing the rule on twenty
  documents before writing it into the plan would have caught it.** I recorded the failure
  and split the verdict by read status rather than re-tuning the threshold, which was the
  right call once the mistake existed — but the mistake was avoidable at the planning step.
- **I misread an HMM header and nearly recorded it.** My `awk` stopped at the first `HMM `
  line, so `all_retron_RT.hmm` looked byte-identical to `clade11-A.hmm` — I was one step
  from writing "the shipped all-retron model is a mislabelled copy of one clade model",
  which would have been a serious and wrong claim about someone's release. It is a
  concatenation of all 29 models. **`md5sum` before the sentence, not after.**
- **Two rounds of slug churn.** I built all 58 pages, saw `notextractedstagec2026_…` slugs,
  rebuilt with curated authors, saw `al2009_`/`ag2024_` slugs, and rebuilt again after
  reading the helper's `_last_name`. Reading 4 lines of `research_wiki.py` first would have
  saved two full rebuilds.
- **Year extraction is still wrong and I stopped fixing it.** `max()` over the opening page
  picked up PDF download stamps (Blocker 2005 → 2026); the fix improved it but still gives
  1999. I stopped and recorded the method per page instead of iterating. Defensible, but
  the wiki carries 35 heuristic years.

## What to fold into the specs

1. **A declared selection rule must be tested on a held-out sample before it is written
   into a plan.** `EVIDENCE_STANDARDS` §5 forbids moving a threshold after scoring — which
   is exactly why the rule has to be shown to discriminate *before* it is committed to.
   Otherwise the honest options at run time are both bad: tune it (forbidden) or report a
   rule that says nothing. Candidate addition to §5.
2. **"Tools agree" needs a lineage check before the sentence is written, not after.**
   §3b says declare lineage; this stage shows the payoff is largest when the declaration
   happens at the point a corroboration claim is drafted. 18 of 32 claims here are
   SHARED_LINEAGE or SELF_REFERENTIAL.
3. **Never derive a person's name from a heuristic.** The surname extractor returned
   "University", "Pipeline" and "Server". A missing attribution is recoverable; a
   fabricated one propagates. Candidate line for `WORKING_AGREEMENT.md`.
4. **Check the writer's own conventions before generating identifiers in bulk.**
   `research_wiki.py` derives slugs from the last whitespace token of the first author.
   Two rebuilds for want of reading one function.
