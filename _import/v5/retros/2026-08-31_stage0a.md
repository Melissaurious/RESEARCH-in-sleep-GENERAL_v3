# Retro — 2026-08-31 · Stage 0a (RETRON-DB V5) · Blind reproduction from primary data

**Outcome:** Q1–Q7 computed exactly over 3,358,182 records / 79.1 GB. 19 artefacts,
`check_prov.py` PASS. ~45 min against a 2–4 h estimate.
**Headline process failure:** the stage was specified as blind and was not blind. See §3.

---

## 1. What worked

**Measuring before sizing.** I timed one real 1.5 GB file end-to-end (5.2 s, 286 MB/s,
10,163 rec/s) before committing to an architecture. That turned "79 GB is scary, maybe
this needs Ibex" into "277 core-seconds, 16 workers, 130 s wall" — and it moved the job
from the >10 min stop-and-ask band into the low-stakes band, which is why there was no
gate. **The measurement decided the machine, the parallelism and the approval path in one
shot.** Guessing would have got all three wrong in the conservative direction.

**One pass, then a cache; every question read the cache.** The corpus was parsed exactly
once into 158 MB of TSV. Q1–Q7 each then ran in 2–6 s. That budget is what made it
affordable to answer every question at *three* units (row / locus / unique protein)
instead of picking one and defending it — which turned out to be the single most
important decision in the stage, because the units disagree by up to 6×.

**Probing fields on real records before designing anything.** Three of the four decisions
that mattered came from the probe, not the schema:
- `is_rt_gene` is the reliable key into `cds_annotations`; `anchor_gene_id` fails to match
  in ~20 % of retron records. Had I keyed on `anchor_gene_id` — the obvious choice from
  the field name — Q3 would have silently lost a fifth of its denominator.
- `system_types` is a one-element list whose single string is *itself* slash-joined. The
  natural `len(system_types) > 1` test for MULTI returns 23 instead of 9,032.
- The two Q3 completeness rules genuinely disagree, which is why the launcher asked for
  the overlap of the sets and not just the rates.

**Validating the cache by content found a data property, not just a bug.**
`02_validate_cache.py` exists to assert widths and invariants. It also reported 174,951
duplicate `rt_system_id` values — a field I had assumed was a unique key. Chasing that
produced the stage's most load-bearing structural result (row ≠ locus; the duplicate axis
is `source_database`). **That is the argument for always writing the validation step, even
when the extraction "obviously worked".**

**Closing an open item instead of filing it.** I had already written the 4,460-record
clipping gap into NOT_COMPUTABLE.md as unexplained. It cost one 6-second script to find
that 4,448 of them are `extended_for_cds`. The lesson is about ordering: draft the
"couldn't compute" list *early*, because seeing an item written down as a defeat is what
prompts the check that resolves it.

## 2. What I misunderstood

**I nearly dropped the column that closed §6.** `extended_for_cds` went into the 36-column
TSV and then got cut from the 25-column parquet as "not needed by any question". It was
needed. I got away with it only because the TSVs were still the cache of record. **The
cost of carrying a column is bytes; the cost of a missing one is a re-read of 79 GB.**

**I recorded only the first motif match position.** By the time
`q4_motif_count_distribution` showed multi-match proteins are common, retaining the other
positions meant a second full pass. Same mistake as the column drop, one step earlier: I
decided what the data would be asked before the questions were fully worked through.
Extraction passes over expensive corpora should be **maximally retentive, not minimally
sufficient.**

**Pandas nullable booleans.** `Int8.eq(1)` yields `NA`, and `NA & bool` raises at reduction
time, not at construction. Cost one failed run. Harden every mask to numpy bool at the
point of creation.

**I treated "unavoidable" as the end of the analysis.** My first write-up called the Q5
anchor "unavoidable and declared" and moved on. Declaring it was necessary but it is not
sufficient, and calling it unavoidable let both halves of the actual failure go
unexamined. That is §3.

## 3. Process failure — the stage was specified as blind and was not blind

**What happened.** The launcher's premise is explicit: *"If you are handed a target you
will hit it, so you are not handed one."* The read boundary was drawn at
`ARIS_OUTPUT/stage0a_blind_repro/inputs/`. But the file staged inside that boundary —
`input_format.md`, which must be read to learn the schema — carried prior-session
**results** in its correction notes: an ncRNA split ("31,030 flagged retron RTs, 29,157
populated, 1,873 = **6.04 %**"), a direction rate ("**94.50 %** upstream"), a null rate
("**97.7 %**"), and a GEM row count. **Q5 is precisely the ncRNA question.** I read the
prior answer to Q5 before computing Q5.

**Why this is a process failure and not bad luck.** The blindness of the stage was
enforced by choosing *which directory* to stage, and not by checking *what was inside the
files staged*. Those are different controls, and only the second one can work: a schema
document is exactly the kind of file that accretes findings over time, because every
correction to a contract is discovered by computing something. The staging step needed a
content check and did not have one. **A read boundary drawn by path cannot make a stage
blind.**

**My own half of it.** I opened `input_format.md` and read it start to finish before
asking whether it contained answers. I had a cheap control available and did not use it:
grep the staged inputs for digit-and-percent patterns *before* reading prose, and if they
appear, either request a stripped copy or read with the anchor declared in advance. I
declared the anchor — but retroactively, after the exposure, which preserves honesty and
discards the blindness.

**What it cost, precisely.** Q5's blindness, and nothing else. Q1–Q4, Q6 and Q7 are
unaffected — no prior value for them was in view. I did not select units, denominators or
filters to approach 6.04 %, and my computed values do not match it: I get 1.93 % flag-only
per record, 1.88 % per locus, **6.24 % per unique protein** on 30,773 flagged proteins
against the prior 6.04 % on 31,030. **The proximity of the per-protein figure is the
problem, not the reassurance** — it is exactly the situation where an anchored analyst
would have stopped adjusting once the number looked familiar, and neither I nor a reader
can now prove from the record that I didn't. That unfalsifiability is the real damage, and
it is why the fix has to be structural rather than a promise to be careful.

**Status of the fix (observed, post-computation).**
`V5/templates/input_format_schema_only.md` now exists, created at 18:51 today — after this
stage ran. It correctly strips the numeric answers, replacing them with qualitative
statements ("null for the large majority of records", "an artifact equal to the null
rate"). ⚠️ **One residual computed value remains at its line 264**: the GEM
`52,505 / 52,515` row count. That one is a property of a metadata file rather than an
answer to Q1–Q7, so its risk is low — but the same rule should apply to it, because "low
risk" is the judgment that put the 6.04 % figure in the file in the first place.

**Audit-trail note.** I read that template *after* every number was computed and frozen;
no value changed, and I appended a dated post-script to FINDINGS.md §0 recording the read
rather than leaving the boundary declaration silently over-broad.

## 4. Proposed additions to `input_format.md`

Five corpus behaviours found in stage 0a that **no trap T1–T13 names**. Written to be
pasted into the `## Notes for ARIS` section in its existing style.

Each states the qualitative property first and carries its count in a trailing
`[stage 0a, 2026-08-31]` tag. **That tag is the strip point**: a schema-only build for a
blind stage removes the bracketed figures and keeps the sentences, which is the control
that was missing in §3. The property is what a reader needs to avoid the trap; the count
is what makes the stage non-blind.

```markdown
- **`rt_system_id` is NOT a unique key (stage 0a, 2026-08-31).** The same id recurs on
  multiple records, and the duplicate axis is `source_database`: one genomic locus mined
  from two databases is retained as two records with identical contig, coordinates and RT
  protein but different `genome_id`, `taxonomy_system` and `phylum`. Deduplicate on
  `rt_system_id` before any per-system count, or state that you are counting records.
  Counting records as loci inflates by ~5 %.
  [stage 0a: 174,951 repeat rows over 3,358,182; RT rows exceed RT loci by 4.86 %]

- **`system_types` encodes multi-family membership INSIDE the string, not as list length
  (stage 0a, 2026-08-31).** The field is almost always a one-element list whose single
  value may itself be slash-joined (`"RVT-GII/RVT-CRISPR"`). A `len(system_types) > 1`
  test for MULTI status therefore returns a near-zero count and silently pools unresolved
  labels into single-family statistics — the exact failure trap T4 warns about. Test for
  `"/"` inside the label. A separate, rarer case does use a genuine two-element list: a
  record filed under two family files at once (e.g. `RVT-UG10` + `Retron`).
  [stage 0a: `/`-joined labels 9,032 records; two-element lists 23 records; 1,025 distinct
  labels of which 41 are single resolved]

- **`taxonomy.taxonomy_system` does not take the documented values (stage 0a,
  2026-08-31).** The observed value set is `ncbi`, `gtdb` and `unknown`. The documented
  `mmseqs2_inferred` **does not occur anywhere in the corpus**, and the undocumented
  `ncbi` is the majority value. Trap T5's value set and its "gtdb = direct,
  mmseqs2_inferred = predicted" dichotomy do not describe this corpus; any branch written
  against `mmseqs2_inferred` is dead code, and any `else` branch treating non-`gtdb` as
  inferred mislabels the `ncbi` majority.
  [stage 0a, over 3,059,700 RT records: ncbi 2,267,726 / gtdb 769,698 / unknown 22,276]

- **Some RT records carry an `rt_gene` but NO `cds_annotations` entry with
  `is_rt_gene == true` (stage 0a, 2026-08-31).** The protein exists in `rt_gene.sequence`,
  but its ORF call is absent from the window's annotation array, so there is no
  `prodigal_metadata` to read: no `partial`, no `start_type`, no RBS fields. Every
  Prodigal-derived statistic therefore has an undefined stratum that must be reported as
  its own denominator, not dropped into the complete or incomplete bucket.
  [stage 0a: 31,504 of 3,059,700 RT records corpus-wide; 3,577 of 665,311 retron records
  = 0.54 %]

- **`anchor_gene_id` is not a reliable key into `cds_annotations[]` (stage 0a,
  2026-08-31).** It fails to match any `gene_id` in the array for a substantial minority
  of records, while the `is_rt_gene` flag matches exactly once in every record that has
  one. Locate the RT CDS by the flag, never by joining on `anchor_gene_id` — the id-join
  looks correct, returns fewer rows, and loses them silently.
  [stage 0a: no match for ~20 % of probed retron records; `is_rt_gene` count was exactly 1
  in 3,028,196 records and 0 in the 31,504 above, never >1]
```

## 5. What to fix

1. **Staging control (highest priority, from §3).** A stage declared blind must have its
   staged inputs **content-checked, not path-checked**. Minimum viable control: grep the
   staged tree for percentages and thousands-separated integers, and refuse to stage a
   file that has them until they are stripped or explicitly waived. The strip-point
   convention in §4 makes this cheap to maintain going forward.
2. **Finish the schema-only template**: remove the residual `52,505 / 52,515` at line 264
   of `templates/input_format_schema_only.md`, and stage *that* file rather than
   `input_format.md` in every future blind stage.
3. **Fold the five bullets of §4 into `input_format.md`** per WORKING_AGREEMENT § Probe
   field semantics — the values win, and the correction gets folded back into the contract.
   Two of the five (`rt_system_id` non-uniqueness, `taxonomy_system` values) change
   denominators, so they are not cosmetic.
4. **Promote to WORKING_AGREEMENT:** an extraction pass over a corpus too expensive to
   re-read should retain every field it touches, not the subset the current questions need.
   Two of this stage's three remaining limitations are the same instance of that rule.
5. **Add to the validation-step template:** assert that any field used as a key is
   actually unique, and report the duplicate axis when it is not. That one check produced
   this stage's structural finding.
6. **Environment note:** `retron_tradicional` has no `orjson` / `msgspec` / `ujson`.
   Stdlib `json` reached 286 MB/s end-to-end, which was ample — no install was needed and
   none was made. Recorded so the next session doesn't reflexively install one.
